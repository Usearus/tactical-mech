# Tutorial steps

What Comms asks, and what the player does. A line with no click plays on its own. A continue line waits in the center until Continue is pressed.

## Fight 1 — Raven and one Scout

1. **We'll start on the battlefield. There are two rounds in a battle. In each round you will get a hand of cards shown below.** Click Continue. Nothing on the board yet.
2. **You'll see there are 3 cards at the moment, because you are fighting with one mech.** Click Continue.
3. **Fight with 2 mechs and get 4 cards. Fight with 3 mechs and get 6 cards.** Click Continue.
4. **In this game you'll discover even more advantages for fighting with a full squad of 3 in battle.** Click Continue.
5. **The yellow icon and number is the cost to use the card.** Click Continue.
6. **Playing this card will cost 1 action, which will lower the action counter on all enemies. When this reaches 0, enemies will attack.** Click Continue.
7. **Play Burst Fire.** Click the Burst Fire card.
8. **This will do 4 damage to any unit within two cells from Raven, and reduce the time before all enemies will act by 1. Click this enemy.** Click the Scout.
9. **Let's ignore the other cards for now. Ending the round will always cost 1 action point, so this enemy will be able to act after we end the round.** Click End round. The Scout acts, then the next round starts.
10. **Play Burst Fire again.** Click Burst Fire.
11. **This attack will reduce the enemy's health to 0. Click the Scout.** Click the Scout. That kill ends the fight.

## Rewards

12. **Winning a battle lets you choose a reward. Upgrades stay with your mechs for the rest of the mission.** Click Continue.
13. **Refresh deals a new set. Skip adds 2 charge instead of taking a reward.** Click Continue.
14. **Pick one reward.** Click a reward card.
15. **Press Continue to take it.** Click Continue.

## Map — Comet joins

16. **Now it's comet's turn. They're in range, now attack.** Comet's turn. Click Attack.
17. **This is the battle prep screen. The yellow grid is where the battle will take place.** Click Continue.
18. **Tactics will change where the battlefield is located. Use this to build your strategy.** Click Continue.
19. **Disadvantaged fights will net you better rewards if you win them. So it may be worth it.** Click Continue.
20. **Let's see what "Flank below" does.** Click Flank below.
21. **The reward rarity after the battle went down, but now it is an "Even fight".** Click Continue.
22. **Change our tactics back to Defense.** Click Defense.
23. **Let's commence this fight with Raven and Comet.** Click Commence.

## Fight 2 — Raven, Comet, and three contacts

24. **You have two mechs now, and can see a badge for each mech that matches the color of their cards.** Click Continue.
25. **Some cards have no cost to use. Play Dart now.** Click Dart.
26. **Attack this contact.** Click a contact under a red tile.
27. **Play Snap Shot.** Click Snap Shot. That card selects Raven.
28. **Click either contact.** Click a contact under a red tile.
29. **Drag this card onto the Overdrive slot. I'll show you how to use overdrive next fight.** Drag Burst Fire onto the Overdrive bar. The other Burst Fire is dealt next round. The bar does not fill yet.
30. **End the round.** Click End round. The last contact attacks, then the next round starts.
31. **Play Burst Fire.** Click Raven's Burst Fire.
32. **Click the contact.** Click the last contact. That kill ends the fight.

## Rewards

33. **Winning a battle lets you choose a reward. Upgrades stay with your mechs for the rest of the mission.** Click Continue.
34. **Refresh deals a new set. Skip adds 2 charge instead of taking a reward.** Click Continue.
35. **Pick one reward.** Click a reward card.
36. **Press Continue to take it.** Click Continue.

## Map — Barrage joins

37. **Barrage's turn. Move Barrage up.** Barrage walks onto the map. Click the highlighted map cell.
38. **Attack the contact.** Click Attack.
39. **All three of you are in this one. Commence.** Click Commence.

## Fight 3 — all three pilots, Overdrive

40. **Here's your overdrive bar.** Click continue.
41. **Every time you end a round, all extra cards in your hand fill this bar.** Click continue.
42. **You can also manually drag cards into the Overdrive Slot to fill the bar.** Click continue.
43. **Drag this card onto the Overdrive slot, which will finish filling the bar.** Drag the highlighted card onto the bar. The last pip lights.
44. **The bar is charged, and will make your cards stronger for the remainder of battle. Activate Overdrive.** Click Activate Overdrive.
45. **If you have empty slots in your hand after activating overdrive you will automatically draw your mechs special overdrive cards.** Click continue.
46. **Play Salvo, Barrage's overdrive card.** Click Salvo.
47. **Salvo will hit every enemy in range. This is perfect positioning.** Click continue.
48. **Select the cell barrage is on to lock in the attack from this location.** Click Barrage's own cell. Salvo hits the pack and the mission ends.
