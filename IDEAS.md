# Card rule ideas

These attacks currently hit one enemy. Their old wording suggested extra rules. The notes below are for later, if we want those rules for real.

## Pin

Suppress used to say it pins an enemy in place.

A pin could be a status that stops that mech from moving on its next action. Decide whether it lasts for the rest of the battle round, or also sticks on the overworld after the fight.

## Armor pierce

Slug used to punch through the target's armor.

Pierce could ignore guard and apply its damage straight to HP. A softer version could spend guard without reducing the damage, so the block is stripped and the health still takes the full hit.

## Spread

Volley used to be a spread of smaller rockets. Buckshot and Slash used to read as wide swings at close range.

Spread could split one card's damage across several enemies in range, or hit each of them for a smaller amount. The player would pick the targets, up to a small cap.

## Burst

Flak used to be a shrapnel burst.

Burst could damage every unit in a small patch, centered on the chosen cell, the way Salvo hits a column in front. Friendly fire is a separate choice for each burst card.
